# K-Lunsar Connect Customer Requirements Form

## Form title
K-Lunsar Connect Customer Requirements and Dashboard Design Review

## Form description
We are improving K-Lunsar Connect for customers, students, teachers, admins and super admins. Please tell us what works, what is confusing, and what should be added. Do not submit passwords, payment card numbers or private transaction proof in this form.

## Questions

1. What best describes you?
   - Customer
   - Student
   - Teacher
   - Admin
   - Super Admin
   - Business partner or other

2. Do you like the dashboard design?
   - Yes
   - Mostly, with some changes
   - No

3. How would you rate the design?
   - 1, Poor
   - 2, Needs work
   - 3, Average
   - 4, Good
   - 5, Excellent

4. Which dashboard tasks do you expect to use most often? (Checkboxes)
   - Browse products and services
   - Place orders
   - Make or verify payments
   - Track delivery
   - Register for a programme
   - View progress and results
   - Manage students or teachers
   - View sales and analytics
   - Manage website content
   - Send messages or support requests

5. What do you like about the current design? (Paragraph)

6. What should be added or changed? (Paragraph)

7. What is difficult, confusing or concerning about the system? (Paragraph)

8. How do you currently buy products, request services or register for training? (Paragraph)

9. Which steps take too long or require repeated work? (Paragraph)

10. What information should be visible immediately on your dashboard? (Paragraph)

11. What features or reports are missing? (Paragraph)

12. Do you have questions about credit, payment timing, balances or business terms? (Paragraph)

13. Which communication method do you prefer?
   - In-app notifications
   - Chat
   - Email
   - Phone or WhatsApp
   - SMS

14. May the K-Lunsar team contact you about your response?
   - Yes
   - No

15. Contact name or email (Optional)

## Recommended settings

- Collect email addresses only if the respondent agrees to follow-up.
- Do not allow respondents to upload payment proofs to this requirements form.
- Enable response notifications for the K-Lunsar product team.
- Export responses to Google Sheets for review and prioritisation.
